Privacy Notice

Pre-launch draft. This is not a final privacy policy or legal advice. Before launch, NOVA MARKET must identify its legal operator, address, privacy contact, hosting providers, payment providers, analytics and all processors, and obtain legal review for every target market.

Current preview

This static preview does not provide customer accounts, a live checkout, payment processing, order submission or an operational contact form. The demonstration cart uses browser-local storage only if future cart persistence is enabled; it is not connected to a server. Hosting infrastructure may process technical request data such as IP address, browser details and security logs.

Information planned for order processing

When the store launches, NOVA MARKET may need customer name, delivery address, contact details, order contents and transaction references to accept and fulfil purchases, provide support, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. Only necessary information should be collected.

Supplier and service-provider sharing

For direct-store fulfilment, necessary delivery details may be shared with approved suppliers, carriers, payment providers and service providers. Before launch, NOVA MARKET must disclose the relevant recipients or recipient categories, purposes, international transfers, safeguards and retention periods, and establish appropriate contracts and legal grounds.

Cookies and analytics

No analytics or advertising tracking service is intentionally configured in this storefront preview. Any future cookies, analytics or marketing tools must be disclosed and consent obtained where required.

Security, retention and rights

Customer data must be protected with access controls, encryption where appropriate, limited retention and incident procedures. Applicable access, correction, deletion, objection and complaint rights will be described in the final policy for each market.
