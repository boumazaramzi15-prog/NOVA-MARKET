AFTER-SALES SUPPORT

Returns & refunds

Pre-launch policy preview — final terms require legal review before sales begin.

Before ordering

Each product page will identify any product-specific return restrictions, hygiene or safety exclusions, warranty terms and return window before checkout.

Requesting a return

When orders are active, customers will be able to contact support with their order reference, reason for return and relevant photos where appropriate. Do not send products to a supplier address until support provides written instructions.

Refunds

Approved refunds will be returned through the original payment method where supported. Processing times depend on the payment provider and financial institution.

Damaged, incorrect or missing items

Contact support promptly with the order reference and evidence so the case can be reviewed with the fulfilment supplier.
