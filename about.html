OUR PURPOSE

About NOVA MARKET

NOVA MARKET is being developed as a global direct-to-customer online store. Customers will browse products, place orders with NOVA MARKET and receive products shipped by approved fulfilment suppliers.

How we plan to work

  1. We review product details, supplier reliability, total cost and destination availability.
  2. We publish product listings with clear prices, delivery information and policies.
  3. Customers place orders through NOVA MARKET checkout once secure payment is enabled.
  4. Approved suppliers prepare and ship orders under NOVA's order-management process.

Current launch status

The storefront is a development preview. Product listings, live checkout, supplier connections and fulfilment are not yet active. Do not submit payment or personal order details through this preview.
