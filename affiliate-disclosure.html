Affiliate disclosure

NOVA MARKET's intended business model is a direct-to-customer store: customers will place orders with NOVA MARKET, and approved fulfilment suppliers may ship products on NOVA MARKET's behalf.

Current status

NOVA MARKET is not using affiliate links as its core business model. No live product listings, affiliate tracking links, checkout or payment processing are active in this preview.

Future changes

If a specific affiliate relationship is ever introduced for a clearly identified section, NOVA MARKET will disclose that relationship next to the relevant link before a customer follows it. Such a limited relationship will not change the direct-store model for NOVA products.
